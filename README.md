# NekoDesu

<img src="Resources/Catgirl/Alice.gif" width="300" height="300" alt="Alice The Mascot Catgirl">   

### *DISCLAIMER: This is a joke app. A meme. A bamboozling, if you will. It was made to poke fun at procrastination.*

## Actual README:

A macOS menu bar cat that watches you not do the thing, then hands you a rank for it.

Sit in Discord long enough and you become **E-Kitten**. After that, flip **I'm procrastinating** and keep not working. Minutes become points. Points become Stray Cat, Street Cat, Alley Cat, House Cat, and finally The Great Old Cat. Ranks only go up. There is no demotion, because the app already knows how this ends.

A pixel catgirl lives in the corner of your screen. She does not help. After a rank-up she has one line, and then she goes back to sitting there.

## What she does

- **Cat Ears Meter.** Discord earns E-Kitten. The toggle earns everything after. You have to admit you're procrastinating. Idling in silence does not count, which is somehow worse.
- **Desk cat.** A pixel cat art, bottom-left, crisp pixels, every Space. Click her after a promotion if you want to be congratulated incorrectly.
- **Clipboard.** She notices you copied something and has an opinion. She keeps the count. She does not keep the text. She will still be disappointed.
- **Honesty pass.** Point her at the frontmost browser page and she translates "we use cookies to improve your experience" into what it actually means. She looks. She does not click Accept for you. That part is still your problem.
- **Optional Discord Rich Presence.** You choose whether you want to be seen while using NekoDesu. Usually the answer's "no" and that's the default. But if you toggle it on and say "yes," meaning you DO want Discord Rich Presence, I'd consider you a freaking masochist. Who in their right mind would want that?!

## What she refuses to do

No accounts. No uploads. No reading your Discord. No recording your microphone. No clicking through cookie walls. No streaks, no shame dialog, no phone app that texts you about your potential.

She lives on your Mac, macOS 13 or later, and she stays there.
